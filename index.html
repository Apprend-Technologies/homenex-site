The ₹-crore leak

Leads don't wait. Neither should you.

Portal & ad enquiries arrive round the clock. By the time an agent replies, the buyer has already messaged four other brokers. The fastest reply almost always wins the deal.

Too slow to respond

You're driving, on a site visit, or asleep. The enquiry sits unanswered for hours — and the buyer moves on to whoever replied first.

78% of buyers pick the first agent to respond

Follow-ups slip away

Hundreds of "call back next week" leads tracked in a notebook or across five WhatsApp chats. Warm buyers go cold because nobody followed up.

Missed site visits

Double-booked slots, no reminders, no-shows. A forgotten site visit is a lost commission — and a builder relationship damaged.

1 in 3 site visits are no-shows

No buyer memory
